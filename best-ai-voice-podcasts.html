Best AI Voice Tools for Podcasts in 2026

Updated May 2026 · 8 min read
If you record yourself: Descript is the best podcast editing tool available. AI removes filler words, fixes mistakes, and edits audio through transcript editing.
If you want fully AI-generated episodes: ElevenLabs for maximum voice quality, Murf AI for editing control.

Two Types of AI Podcast Tools

AI is used in podcasting two different ways, and the right tool depends on which approach you're taking.

Type 1 — AI-assisted editing: You record yourself, AI helps edit. Tools like Descript transcribe your audio, remove filler words automatically, let you cut sections by editing text, and fix mistakes without re-recording.

Type 2 — Fully AI-generated: You write a script, AI narrates it. No recording required. ElevenLabs and Murf AI generate professional audio from text alone.

Best for Editing Your Own Recordings: Descript

Try Descript Free →

Descript is the standout tool for podcasters who record themselves. The transcript-based editing workflow is genuinely transformative — edit your audio the same way you'd edit a Word document. Cut a sentence by deleting it. Rearrange sections by cutting and pasting paragraphs.

The AI filler word removal feature alone saves significant time for most podcasters. One click identifies and removes every "um", "uh", "like", and "you know" from a recording. Review the suggestions, approve, done.

Overdub lets you fix recording mistakes by typing the correction. The AI regenerates just that word or phrase in your voice — listeners can't tell the difference.

Pricing: Free (1 hour/month) · Creator $12/month · Pro $24/month

Best for Fully AI-Generated Podcasts: ElevenLabs

Try ElevenLabs Free →

For a completely AI-narrated podcast, ElevenLabs produces the most natural-sounding audio available. Long-form content is where voice quality matters most — listeners spend 30-60 minutes per episode, and a voice that sounds slightly robotic becomes fatiguing quickly.

Voice cloning is the key feature for AI podcasts. Clone your own voice once, and every episode sounds like you — without recording. Alternatively, choose from 1,000+ pre-built voices to find the right character for your show.

Pricing: Free (10,000 chars) · Starter $5/month · Creator $22/month

Best for Editing Control: Murf AI

Try Murf AI Free →

If you're producing fully AI-narrated podcasts and need precise control over how the narration sounds, Murf AI's editor is the advantage. Adjust pronunciation, emphasis, and pacing on individual words without regenerating the full episode. For polished, professional-sounding AI podcasts, this editing control is valuable.

Pricing: Free (10 min/month) · Basic $19/month · Pro $26/month

Can AI Podcasts Succeed?

Yes — fully AI-narrated podcasts have built substantial audiences across finance, technology, history, and self-improvement categories. The key factors are script quality and voice naturalness. With ElevenLabs-quality audio and well-written scripts, listeners often cannot tell the difference from human narration.

The practical advantage: AI podcast production takes a fraction of the time of recorded podcasts. A 30-minute episode can be produced in 2-3 hours including scripting, generation, and basic editing — compared to 6-10 hours for a recorded show including recording, editing, and post-production.
